# Sint Lucia Instituut, Rotterdam

## Identity and chronology

The requested building is the former Buskens convent and school complex at Aert van Nesstraat, corner Hennekijnstraat, later the site of Rotterdam Building. Do not conflate it with the 1957-1958 Jos and Leo de Jonge school above Garage Ben Maltha behind the convent, nor with the Sint Lucia institution near the Boschjeskerk, nor with the Bennebroek motherhouse.

Platform Wederopbouw Rotterdam states that the new Buskens complex opened in 1920 and survived the 1940 bombardment. It needed extra teaching accommodation after the war. This specialist account is preferable to the conflicting TOP010 statement that it was mostly destroyed. Source: https://wederopbouwrotterdam.nl/artikelen/garage-ben-maltha-kweekschool-sint-lucia/

Flickr collection descriptions state a L-shaped plan, high wings with expressionist brick ornament, a central chapel whose apse faced a small courtyard, L.C. van der Vlugt assisting Buskens, and demolition in 1972. The Flickr scan upload dates in 2014 are NOT original photograph dates. Source: https://www.flickr.com/photos/jansluijter/15620696661

BONAS independently describes the school and convent wings as sculptural architecture inspired by the Amsterdam School. Source: https://www.bonas.nl/archiwijzer/gegevens.php?inr=0146.00000
