-1930 photograph is preferred street facade evidence for1939: broad brick plane, large TIVOLI letters, central projecting billboard above broad shallow canopy, symmetrical upper windowband and vertical decorative strips.1940 damage photo04-facade1940-original.jpg is research-only and shows parapet silhouette but missing roof/windows; do not reconstruct damage as1939.
